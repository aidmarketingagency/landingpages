What the agent does

It picks up the calls and chats your team can’t get to and asks the questions your team already asks: buy or sell, timeline, pre-approval, areas and best contact time. It books on your calendar, then posts each lead it captures into your Follow Up Boss so your own action plan fires.

Before any agent goes live, it has to pass tests for capture, routing, duplicate messages, handoff to a person and failure alerts. If one fails, it doesn’t go out.

Your order names the phone number, chat entry point, calendar, alert recipient and CRM routing rule the agent covers. It covers the inquiries it captures. It doesn’t work leads already sitting in your Follow Up Boss; the teaching session is built for that. And texting a lead that lands in your CRM first isn’t part of this today.

Bonus: comment-to-DM on your listing and open-house posts, once it passes a test on your own Page permissions.
