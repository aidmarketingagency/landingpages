Sunday, 7:30 PM

A buyer fills out the form on your listing at 7:30 on a Sunday night. Your best agent is at a showing. Your next best is at dinner with the phone face down. The inquiry lands in the CRM and sits there.

It shows up in the data too. Mike DelPrete ran a secret shop of 100+ shops across 25+ brokerages in August 2024, and 47% of online property inquiries got no response at all. For the ones that did, the median first reply took 39 minutes. And 62% of open-house visitors got no follow-up.

It matters because buyers don't wait politely. Zillow's December 2025 report found that 47% of buyers and 59% of sellers hired the first agent they actually spoke with.

Two different 47% numbers sit on this page. The first is inquiries that got no response. The second is buyers who hired the first agent they spoke with. Both are sourced at the bottom of the page.
