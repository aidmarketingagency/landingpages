faq:
- question: Is AI adoption common among small businesses today?
  answer: Yes, AI adoption has grown significantly among small businesses. According to the U.S. Chamber of Commerce (2025), 58% of small businesses now use generative AI, up from 40% in 2024 and 23% in 2023. This trend reflects a broader shift toward using technology to handle routine administrative burdens.
- question: How does AI specifically benefit field-service companies?
  answer: AI improves field operations by enhancing asset uptime, reducing service costs, and improving customer experience. Research from Geotab (2025) shows that 88% of field-service companies report these positive outcomes from using new technology. These gains often stem from better data visibility before the technician arrives on-site.
- question: What is the most effective way to start using AI in a service business?
  answer: The most realistic first step involves using AI for operational support tasks like writing estimates and summarizing calls. By automating these low-value administrative duties, business owners can focus more on their trade. A missed-call text-back is a practical starting point for most service businesses.
- question: Do small business owners view AI as a reliable tool?
  answer: Many owners have moved past the novelty phase and now view AI as a business dependency. In fact, 77% of small businesses using AI report that limits on the technology would negatively impact their growth and bottom line (U.S. Chamber of Commerce, 2025). This reliance highlights how quickly these tools integrate into daily workflows.
- question: When should a Charlotte business owner hire an AI consultant?
  answer: A consultant makes sense when a business owner has identified specific bottlenecks but lacks the time or technical knowledge to evaluate and deploy the right tools. The goal is to avoid wasting weeks testing software that does not fit the workflow. A good consultant shortens that learning curve and focuses on systems that produce measurable time savings.
