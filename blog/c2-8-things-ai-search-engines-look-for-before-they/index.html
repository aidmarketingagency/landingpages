faq:
- question: Why does AI only recommend a small percentage of local businesses?
  answer: AI models prioritize high-confidence data to ensure they provide the most
    helpful answers. They filter out businesses with inconsistent information, poor
    reviews, or low web authority. According to the SOCi Local Visibility Index, ChatGPT
    recommends only 1.2 percent of all local business locations.
- question: How can I get my business featured on expert-curated lists?
  answer: Start by building relationships with local media and industry trade groups.
    Focus on providing unique value or expertise that makes your business a natural
    fit for these lists, and ensure your business is active in local community events
    and trade associations.
- question: Do AI search engines care more about review volume or review recency?
  answer: Both metrics are important, but recency is a major signal for building trust
    with AI systems. A business with a large volume of old reviews may appear less
    relevant than one with a steady stream of fresh, high-quality feedback. AI models
    prioritize current data to ensure the business is still active and reliable.
- question: Why are dedicated service pages important for AI visibility?
  answer: AI models need to map specific user needs to precise solutions. Dedicated
    service pages allow the AI to crawl your site and understand exactly what you
    offer. This structure makes it significantly easier for the model to match your
    business to a specific user query.
- question: How do I make my business easier for AI to cite in search results?
  answer: Structure your website so that your business name, address, phone number,
    and service areas are clearly labeled. Use clear headings, consistent contact
    information, and specific service pages to ensure the AI can easily extract the
    information it needs to recommend you.
