Find out how fast your team answers, in 20 minutes.

The Speed Check is a free 20-minute online call for real estate team leaders, run by me, Ryan McNally of AID Marketing Agency in Charlotte, North Carolina. You share your CRM dashboard, we read your own response-time numbers together, and I show you a 60-second clip of the workflow. Then I tell you plainly what the paid session is and what it costs, and you tell me whether you’d rather learn to run it or have it done for you. It works for teams anywhere in the country, and Follow Up Boss is where it’s easiest to see.

What happens, in order

First I thank you, say what we’ll cover, and ask for permission to look at your dashboard. If you want the call recorded, I’ll ask before I press record.

Then you share your CRM dashboard. In Follow Up Boss, we read Speed to Action and Unactioned leads. In another CRM, we open its response-time report. If your CRM doesn’t have one, we open your inbox and time your most recent new inquiries by hand.

Then I ask one question: what happens to a new inquiry that lands at 7:30 on a Sunday? You answer it with what’s on your screen.

Who should come

The team leader or the budget owner, with your CRM admin if you can bring them.

What I won’t do

I won’t set anything up during the call or build you a private demo beforehand, and I won’t make claims about your numbers beyond what’s on your screen.

Questions people ask before they book

Is it really free?

Yes. It’s 20 minutes. I’ll say what the paid session is and what it costs near the end, and you’re free to say no.

What do I need to bring?

A screen share of your CRM dashboard. If you can bring your CRM admin, bring them. If your CRM doesn’t report response time, we’ll time your recent inquiries by hand.

What if I’m not on Follow Up Boss?

Come anyway. Lofty has its own response-time reports, BoldTrail, KW Command and Cloze work with a hand count, and for Compass, Howard Hanna Engage and Realty ONE we check what your account can do on your screen.

Will you set something up or pitch me hard?

I won’t set anything up. I do say the price plainly near the end, because you should know what it is before you decide.

Do you record it?

Only if you say yes first. I ask at the start.
