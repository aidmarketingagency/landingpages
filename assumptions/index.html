What this draft assumes

This is version two of the rebuilt site. Nothing here is live, and the live site at aidmarketingagency.com is unchanged. Each item below names the decision it waits on.

What changed from V1

  • Ryan reviewed V1 on October 14 and said it was out of alignment, wordy, had no animation, overexplained the method, and showed pricing to the whole world.
  • Every price is gone from the public pages. Pricing is shared on the Speed Check call and on a private checkout link.
  • The method is gone too: no session breakdown, no CRM menu paths, no template text, no scoreboard mechanics. The pages show outcomes.
  • Copy is cut to roughly a third, and it leads with how the result feels.
  • The home page opens with an animated storyboard of a Sunday-night inquiry getting answered, plus two short animated graphics built on sourced numbers.
  • The hero is realigned to one left edge with more room between the words and the picture.
