I’m Ryan McNally. I teach teams to answer leads faster.

I’m the founder of AID Marketing Agency in Charlotte, North Carolina. I help real estate teams answer new inquiries fast, online, anywhere in the country.

I got here sideways. I spent five years as a home inspector, working next to realtors every week, and I was the tech guy: 360 cameras, automated emails, even the Google Business page. (Yes, the inspector built that.) Then came five years in sales. Now I’m in field sales in Charlotte.

Along the way I built AI to fix my own problems first. It handles my end-of-day paperwork and covers whatever comes in while I’m out in the field.

I’m a Charlotte boy dad. I was raised in South Jersey, so the Eagles come with the birth certificate. Go Birds.
