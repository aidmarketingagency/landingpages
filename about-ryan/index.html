I’m Ryan McNally. I teach teams to answer leads faster.

I’m the founder of AID Marketing Agency, based in Charlotte, North Carolina. I teach real estate teams to answer new inquiries fast, and I run the work online for teams anywhere in the country.

I got here sideways. I spent five years as a home inspector, helping my dad build his franchise and working next to realtors every week. I did more than inspect. I was the business-development and tech guy: running 360 cameras and DocuSketch, automating our emails, building and running our Google Business page. (Yes, I was the inspector who built the Google Business page.)

Then came five years in roofing sales on wind and hail claims. That’s where I watched pipelines die the day a rep quit, and past customers go years without a call. The paperwork lived in trucks and inboxes.

Now I’m in field sales in Charlotte.

Along the way I built AI to fix my own problems first. All of it self taught, none of it from a textbook. Mine handles my end-of-day paperwork and picks up what I can’t while I’m out in the field. That’s why I teach it the way I do: short sessions, real work, a scoreboard.

Off the clock I’m a Charlotte boy dad: jiu jitsu, beach weekends and whatever my boys are into this month. I was raised in South Jersey, so the Eagles come with the birth certificate. Go Birds.
